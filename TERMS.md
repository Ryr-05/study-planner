# Terms and privacy
These are the same plain-language terms shown in the app (Me > Terms and privacy).

## Who Noctil is for

Noctil is a planner for college students who are 18 or older. By using it, you confirm that you are 18 or older.

## Your data stays yours

Your tasks, classes, habits and focus sessions are saved on this phone. If you sign in with Google, they are also synced to your own account so your other devices can see them. You can back up, export or delete everything from Me.

## Noctil AI is optional

When you use Noctil AI with AI on, Noctil sends Google's Gemini service what you type to it, plus your open tasks (titles, due dates, times, start times, priority, list, tags and length), your class timetable (class names, days and times, not rooms) and your habits (names, goals and streaks). Notes, checklists, rooms and places are never sent. It can add tasks and habits and change tasks when you ask, with Undo; it changes a deadline only when you ask for that, and only after you confirm. It can't delete anything. Your chats are saved on this phone for 7 days and you can clear them anytime. Gemini is used through Google's free service, so Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and trained reviewers may read it. Please don't put private details in tasks or messages. AI stays off until you turn it on, and Noctil AI still works in a simpler offline mode without it. If Gemini is busy or out of free messages, the same request may instead be answered by Groq (open models such as gpt-oss) or Cloudflare Workers AI, through Noctil's own relay on Cloudflare; they receive the same information, it isn't stored by the relay, and their own terms apply. Gemini isn't offered in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.

## Plans are suggestions

Plans, reminders and AI answers can be wrong. Always check due dates with your class. Noctil is provided as is, without any warranty.

## Free, no ads

Noctil is free, shows no ads and never sells your data.
